PROMPT — HOVER ANIMATION / GOTHIC SOULSBORNE UI

Animate a dark gothic website interface inspired by gothic fantasy action RPG menus and
Victorian cathedral atmosphere. Keep the original composition, proportions, typography,
grid, text, and colors unchanged.

Hover interaction:
- the hovered button/card rises by 2–3 px;
- scale only very slightly, around 1.01x;
- a thin warm ivory highlight appears along the bottom edge;
- the border becomes subtly brighter;
- the card receives a soft localized glow, not a neon glow;
- image areas slowly push in about 3–4% and become a little clearer;
- the image must remain dark, monochrome, smoky, desaturated, with warm ivory highlights;
- add extremely subtle film grain and drifting dust;
- use slow, elegant easing, 220–320 ms for UI response;
- the image zoom should be slower than the button movement;
- no bounce, no cartoon motion, no flashy cyberpunk effects;
- movement should feel like an old cursed manuscript / cathedral mechanism reacting to touch;
- when the pointer leaves, return smoothly to the exact resting state.

Aesthetic:
Bloodborne / Dark Souls / gothic cathedral / occult manuscript / forgotten shrine /
blackened stone / candlelight reflected on worn metal / aristocratic decay.

Color palette:
background #281f1f and #1d1d1d;
primary text #f5e3c2;
highlight/glow #fcf0d9.

Animation should remain tasteful, restrained and premium, with strong darkness and very
limited warm highlights.
